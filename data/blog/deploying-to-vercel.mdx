Your portfolio only helps you if recruiters can open it. Here is the fastest way I've found.

1. **Push your code to GitHub.** Make the repo public so recruiters can read your code too.
2. **Import it on Vercel.** Sign in with GitHub at vercel.com, choose _Add New → Project_ and pick the repo. Vercel detects Next.js automatically.
3. **Deploy.** Every push to `main` now redeploys the site, and every pull request gets a preview link.
4. **Add a custom domain (optional).** A `yourname.dev` or `yourname.in` domain looks great on a résumé.

## Checklist before you share the link

- [ ] Your real name, photo and links in `data/siteMetadata.js`
- [ ] At least three projects with screenshots and live links
- [ ] A short About page that says what you're looking for
- [ ] Lighthouse score above 90 on mobile
